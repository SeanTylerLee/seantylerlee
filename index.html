Permit Path

iOS · Android · Website

Oversize route navigation you control. Build the permit-approved path with unlimited waypoints, then navigate that exact route — without reading the permit behind the wheel.

  • Unlimited waypoints for long permit corridors
  • Plan on one device, navigate on another
  • Share the same route across iOS and Android
Website

02

HaulPath

iOS · Android · Website

Enter load dimensions, pick the states on your route, and see what’s required before you roll — permits, pilot cars, and high poles, state by state.

  • 48-state oversize and overweight rules
  • Pilot car and high-pole thresholds at a glance
  • Works offline when you need the answer now
Website

03

Pilot Car 4 Hire

iOS · Website

A free nationwide pilot car directory. Carriers tap a state and call the escort. Drivers list for free. No fees, no middleman, no paywall.

  • Browse lead, chase, hi-pole, steereman, route survey, and flagger
  • No account needed to search or contact
  • Listing is free — always
