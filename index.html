Permit Path

iOS · Android · Website

Oversize route navigation you control. Build the permit path with unlimited waypoints, or import a Texas TxPROS permit and drive the exact route the TxPROS website issued.

  • Unlimited waypoints for long permit corridors
  • Import a TxPROS permit from the PDF or the QR code
  • The imported line is the exact route from the TxPROS website
  • Plan on one device, navigate on another
Website

HaulPath

iOS · Android · Website

Enter load dimensions, pick the states on your route, and see what’s required before you roll — permits, pilot cars, and high poles, state by state.

  • 48-state oversize and overweight rules
  • Pilot car and high-pole thresholds at a glance
  • Works offline when you need the answer now
Website

Pilot Car 4 Hire

iOS · Android · Website

A free nationwide pilot car directory. Carriers tap a state and call the escort. Drivers list for free. No fees, no middleman, no paywall.

  • Browse lead, chase, hi-pole, steereman, route survey, and flagger
  • No account needed to search or contact
  • Listing is free — always
Website

Story

Built for the job, then kept running.

Oversize freight runs on a stack of state rules, a route the permit actually allows, and a pilot car when the load needs one. We built an app for each of those, because the paper and the phone calls were the job.

HaulPath is the check before the truck moves. Permit Path is the path you are allowed to drive. In Texas, import the TxPROS permit and the app loads the exact route from the TxPROS website. Pilot Car 4 Hire is the free call list when an escort has to be on the move. Same company. Three different jobs. All live on iOS, Android, and the web.

STL Apps LLC designs the apps, ships them, and keeps them updated for people who use them on the haul.
