Information we handle on this Site

1. Information you send us

If you email seantylerlee@icloud.com or otherwise contact us, we receive whatever you include — typically your email address, name if you give one, and the content of your message. We use that only to reply, evaluate a project or support request, and keep a record of the conversation as needed to run the business.

The automatic quote tool on this Site runs in your browser. We do not store the options you pick as an account on this Site. If you use “Save quote” or “I’m interested,” a PDF of the estimate is downloaded to your device and a copy of that quote (including your name, email, and project notes if you entered them) is sent to STL Apps LLC so we can reply.

2. Technical and hosting data

The Site is a static website. Hosting and your browser may record ordinary technical data so the Site can be served and stay online, such as IP address, browser and device type, pages requested, date and time, and referral URL. We do not use this Site to build marketing profiles or sell advertising.

3. No accounts on this Site

This Site does not ask you to create an account, does not store personal profiles, and does not process payments.

4. FleetDispatch demo

The interactive FleetDispatch demo on this Site is a local, in-browser simulation. Sample names, loads, and tickets shown there are fictional. The demo is not a live company system and is not intended to collect your personal information. If your browser stores temporary demo state, that stays on your device and is not sent to STL Apps LLC as an account.
