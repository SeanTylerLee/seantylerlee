Privacy Policy

STL Apps for Mac · Last updated September 3, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how STL Apps LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in the STL Apps Mac application (the “App”). The App is a personal studio-operations tool. It is not a public social product and it does not create an account with us on a server.

This page is for the Mac App only. The company website (seantylerlee.com) and other products (Permit Path, HaulPath, Pilot Car 4 Hire) have their own practices.

This page is provided for general informational purposes and is not legal advice.

Who we are

STL Apps LLC owns and operates the App. Questions: seantylerlee@icloud.com

What the App stores

Almost everything you type or attach in the App stays on your Mac, in the App’s local database and (for some secrets) in macOS Keychain. We do not run a cloud account for this App. We cannot see your projects, invoices, passwords, or receipts unless you send them to us yourself.

That local data can include:

  • Business information, clients, leads, projects, notes, to-dos, and calendar notes
  • Invoices, quotes, contracts, receipts, and related PDFs
  • Income, expenses, owner draws, taxes, inventory, and attached photos or PDFs
  • App records, support tickets, known issues, SOPs, and email lists you build
  • Login vault entries (including passwords you choose to store)
  • Bank connection settings, such as a Mercury API token if you paste one
  • App Store Connect or Google Play credentials if you paste them for analytics

Keychain and secrets

Sensitive values (for example bank routing and account numbers, portal passwords, API tokens, and store keys) are stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac when the App offers that. They are not uploaded to STL Apps LLC.

Backups

If you use File → Save Backup, the App writes a .stlappsbackup package to a folder you choose. That package can include the database, company documents, settings, and secrets. Anyone with that file can restore your studio. Keep backups private (external drive or a folder only you control).

Network use

The App only talks to the internet when you ask it to, for example:

  • Mercury (bank balances and transactions), if you paste an API token
  • Apple App Store Connect or Google Play, if you add those credentials for stats
  • Opening a website you saved (login vault, SOP links, store consoles)
  • Mail, if you choose to send an invoice reminder or similar from the App

Those services have their own privacy policies. We do not sell or share App data with advertisers. The App does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App can remind you about renewals and similar due dates on this Mac. Those alerts are scheduled on your device.

Files and other apps on your Mac

Attachments (receipts, invoices, icons, documents) are files you pick or drop in. Backups and exports go where you choose in the save panel. If you import a Numbers spreadsheet, the App may ask Numbers to export a CSV so it can read the list. You can refuse those prompts.

What we receive if you contact support

If you email seantylerlee@icloud.com, we receive whatever you send (your address, the message, and any attachments). We use that to reply and keep a record of the request.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not store information about children in the App if you are using it as a business tool.

Your choices

  • Do not paste a token, password, or bank number you do not want stored on this Mac.
  • Delete a record inside the App when you no longer need it.
  • Delete a backup file you created.
  • Remove the App from this Mac. Local App data in Application Support and related Keychain items can be deleted with the App or by you in Finder / Keychain Access.

We do not hold a copy of your studio on our servers, so we cannot remotely wipe your Mac. If you emailed us, you can ask us to delete that email thread.

Data requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. For data inside the App, you already have it on your Mac. For emails you sent us, write seantylerlee@icloud.com.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
